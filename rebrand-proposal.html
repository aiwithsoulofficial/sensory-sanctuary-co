01

The opportunity you're sitting on

Sensory Sanctuary Co has something that almost no competitor in Australia has: two Occupational Therapists as founders, a model with lived anxiety as the face of the brand, and a product line that genuinely works. What's missing isn't the product - it's the positioning.

Right now, the website reads like a beautiful jewellery store. The problem? You're not selling jewellery. You're selling nervous system regulation tools that happen to look like jewellery. That distinction is everything - and it's nowhere on the site.

The core issue: The words anxiety, ADHD, sensory processing, and neurodivergent don't appear on your homepage. Your biggest competitor - Worry Rings - says "manage your anxiety before you burn out" right in their hero. Your OT credentials are buried on an about page. Rachel's story isn't told at all. The people who need your product can't find it because you're not speaking their language.

The pattern that predicts growth

Across every competitor, the same pattern holds: founder story + face of brand = growth. Worry Rings (124K followers) has Maddyson front and centre. CONQUERing (175K customers) has Tammy's mother-son story. Stimm Jewelry has the best clinical positioning in the market - and 151 followers. Because nobody knows who's behind it.

Rachel is the unlock. A model who lives with anxiety, co-designing regulation tools with an OT? That's not a brand story - that's a movement. It's more authentic than any competitor in the space. But right now, nobody knows she exists.

The positioning nobody owns

Every competitor says "anxiety relief" or "stress management." Not one uses nervous system regulation, co-regulation, or sensory processing language in their hero messaging. That's your lane. You're not selling anxiety rings - you're selling nervous system tools designed by the clinicians who understand them.

03

The repositioned brand

We're not starting from scratch. The products are beautiful, the clinical credibility is real, and the founder story is unmatched. What we're doing is making sure the world can actually see it.

New positioning statement

"The only sensory jewellery in Australia designed by registered Occupational Therapists. Discreet fidget rings and regulation tools for adults with anxiety, ADHD, and sensory processing needs - designed to be beautiful enough that nobody knows it's therapy."

What we build together

Phase 1: Brand repositioning & website

  • Complete brand positioning strategy - ICP definition, messaging framework, competitor differentiation
  • New website design and build - repositioned homepage, product pages, founder story, science section
  • GEO/SEO optimisation - AI search keywords, schema markup, meta tags, alt text strategy

Phase 2: Social media brand system

  • 8-pillar content strategy mapped to buyer journey

  • Content direction guide - what Rachel films, how Caitlin contributes, posting cadence

  • TikTok-first content plan (this is where the market is growing fastest)
  • Hashtag strategy and community engagement playbook

Phase 3: Growth foundations

  • Email/SMS capture setup with welcome sequence
  • Review collection system (social proof is critical)
  • Bundle strategy for average order value growth
  • NDIS eligibility research (opens government-funded sales channel)
  • GEO strategy for AI search engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude) - this is where search is heading
