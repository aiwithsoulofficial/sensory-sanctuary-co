01 / Brand Essence

Who we are

Sensory Sanctuary Co is an Australian sensory jewellery brand founded by two Occupational Therapists - Rachel and Caitlin. We design feminine, elegant fidget rings, spinner necklaces, and digital nervous system regulation tools for adults with anxiety, ADHD, autism, and sensory processing needs.

Our pieces are beautiful - celestial spinners, zircon-accented bands, delicate gold and silver finishes. They're designed so nobody knows they're therapeutic tools unless you tell them. That's the point.

Sensory Sanctuary Co makes beautiful, feminine jewellery that quietly regulates your nervous system. Designed by Occupational Therapists for adults who need sensory support that doesn't look like it came from a therapy catalogue.

Brand pillars

Pillar 01

Beauty first

These are jewellery pieces first, regulation tools second. Every design is feminine, elegant, and something you'd choose to wear even without the therapeutic benefit.

Pillar 02

OT credibility

Every product is informed by occupational therapy principles for sensory processing and nervous system regulation. We don't just sell - we educate.

Pillar 03

Lived experience

Rachel lives with anxiety. She's not just the co-founder - she's the first customer. This brand exists because she couldn't find what she needed.

Colour usage rules

Always

  • Sanctuary Sage as the primary brand colour - headers, buttons, backgrounds
  • Warm Gold for accents, highlights, and the script font
  • Soft Cream as the default page background
  • Charcoal for body text (never pure black)
  • Blush sparingly for warmth in feminine-forward content

Never

  • Pure black (#000000) anywhere - too harsh, use Charcoal
  • Neon or bright colours - this brand is soft, grounded, natural
  • More than 3 colours in a single graphic
  • Gold on gold or sage on sage - always ensure contrast
  • Clinical blue or medical white - we're not a pharmacy
03 / Typography

Fonts

Four fonts. Each has a role. The script brings femininity and luxury. The display brings editorial elegance. The serif adds warmth to longer text. The sans-serif keeps everything clean.

Script / Accent - Pinyon Script
Sensory Sanctuary Co
Use for: Logo lockup, hero accent text, section highlights, pull quotes. Never use for body copy or paragraphs. Minimum size: 24px.
Display / Headings - Italiana

04 / Brand Voice

How we sound

Warm, knowing, validating, direct. We speak to women who are already overwhelmed - our job is to make their nervous system relax, not give them more to process.

Voice spectrum

✓ We are

  • Warm - like a friend who gets it, not a brand selling at you
  • Knowing - we understand the mask, the overwhelm, the secret fidgeting
  • Direct - we say anxiety, ADHD, sensory processing. We don't hide behind "wellness"
  • Beautiful - our words feel as considered as our designs
  • Grounded - clinical credibility expressed with softness
  • Normalising - you're not broken, you're overstimulated

✗ We are not

  • Clinical - no medical jargon, no white-coat energy
  • Vague - no "calm vibes" or "gentle sanctuary" without context
  • Cutesy - not infantilising. These are adult women.
  • Corporate - no brand-speak, no "leveraging synergies"
  • Preachy - no toxic positivity, no "just breathe"
  • Dramatic - no catastrophising, no fear-based marketing

Language swaps

Specific words carry positioning. These swaps ensure every piece of copy reinforces who we are.

Don't say Say instead Why
stress relief nervous system regulation We own regulation language - nobody else does
wellness jewellery sensory jewellery / regulation tools "Wellness" is saturated. Sensory is specific.
fidget toy sensory ring / fidget ring "Toy" sounds childish. Ring sounds premium.
calming grounding / regulating Calming is passive. Regulating is active.
inspired by therapy designed by Occupational Therapists Specific credentials, not vague inspiration
for everyone for adults with anxiety, ADHD, and sensory needs When you speak to everyone, you reach nobody
subtle / discreet (as main message) beautiful first, therapeutic second Lead with beauty, not with hiding
self-care self-regulation Self-care is a bath bomb. Self-regulation is a skill.

Tagline options

Beautiful jewellery. Quiet regulation.

Alternatives:

  • "Jewellery your nervous system chose for you."
  • "Designed by OTs. Worn by women who look fine."
  • "The ring you reach for when everything is too much."
  • "Regulate in secret. Shine in public."
05 / Ideal Customer

Who we're talking to

We have three core customer profiles. Every piece of content, every product description, every ad should speak to one of these women.

Primary

The masking professional

25-45. Corporate or client-facing role. Has anxiety or ADHD (diagnosed or suspected). Picks her nails, bounces her leg, clicks pens. Needs something beautiful and invisible. Wants to regulate without anyone noticing.

She searches for: anxiety ring, fidget ring for work, ADHD jewellery, discreet fidget ring

Secondary

The overwhelmed mum

28-45. Running on adrenaline. Kids, school, activities, sensory overload everywhere. Possibly neurodivergent herself, possibly has a neurodivergent child. Needs grounding in the chaos. Buys for herself AND as gifts for her daughter.

She searches for: anxiety ring for daughter, sensory gifts, nervous system support, mum burnout

Tertiary

The gift-giver

Any age. Looking for a meaningful gift for someone who struggles with anxiety, sensory processing, or overwhelm. Wants something that says "I see you" without being patronising. The OT design story gives her confidence it's not just a pretty ring.

She searches for: anxiety gift, gift for anxious friend, sensory jewellery gift, meaningful gift for her

06 / Photography & Imagery

Visual direction

The imagery should feel like a luxury fashion editorial crossed with real life. Beautiful but accessible. Styled but not staged. Rachel is the face - and she should look like herself, not a catalogue model.

Photography style

Product close-ups
Soft natural light. Macro details on the ring mechanisms. Show the spin, the texture, the sparkle. Cream/sage backgrounds.

Rachel wearing
Natural light, candid energy. Wearing the ring at a cafe, in a meeting, at the beach. Beautiful but real. Not posed.

Hands & detail
Close-ups of hands wearing the rings in real settings. Coffee cup, keyboard, steering wheel. Show the ring IN LIFE.

Flat lays & lifestyle
Ring on a journal, next to a cup of tea, on a marble tray. Feminine, curated, aspirational but warm.

Photography rules

Always

  • Natural light - golden hour, soft window light, outdoor shade
  • Warm tones - cream, sage, blush, gold in backgrounds
  • Show real skin, real hands, real settings
  • Rachel's face in at least 30% of all content
  • Show the ring being USED (spinning, touching) not just worn

Never

  • Flash photography or harsh studio lighting
  • Stock photos of any kind - only Rachel, Caitlin, and real customers
  • Clinical or medical settings
  • Faceless product shots only - people sell, not things
  • Over-filtered or heavily edited skin

Rachel as face of brand

Rachel is the brand. Her face, her story, her anxiety - that's what makes this different from every other fidget ring on the internet. She's a model who lives with anxiety, co-designing regulation tools with an OT. People need to see her, hear her, know her. Every scroll of the Instagram grid should show a woman they want to be - beautiful, honest, and unapologetically managing her nervous system.

Instagram account strategy

If Rachel converted her personal account to @sensorysanctuaryco, the existing followers were never there for sensory jewellery. The algorithm sees the content pivot, engagement drops, reach tanks. Recommendation: Create a fresh @sensorysanctuaryco brand account. Rachel's personal stays personal but becomes the engine - tagging the brand, wearing the product, telling her story. Personal drives traffic. Brand converts sales.
