Usage Notes

  • Size: These are at Instagram's 4:5 ratio (1080 x 1350px). Screenshot each at 2x for crisp quality, or recreate in Canva/Figma at full resolution.
  • Rachel's photos: Covers 01 (Founder Story) and any lifestyle content should feature Rachel wearing the rings. Natural light, candid energy - not stock-photo posed.
  • Caitlin's photos: Cover 07 (OT Explains) features Caitlin. Professional but warm - the credibility anchor.
  • Consistency: Every reel gets a cover from one of these 8 categories. When someone lands on the grid, they instantly see a brand - not random content.
  • Content pillars: These 8 covers map to 8 content pillars. Rotate through them for a balanced grid.

Content Pillar Strategy

  • 01 Founder Story: Rachel's anxiety journey, behind the scenes, day in my life, the moment that started it all
  • 02 How It Works: Close-up of the ring spinning, ASMR sounds, unboxing, showing the mechanism
  • 03 Who It's For: "This ring is for the woman who..." hooks, relatable scenarios, "POV" content
  • 04 The Science: Nervous system education, sensory processing explained, why tactile input works
  • 05 Customer Story: Testimonials, DM screenshots (with permission), before/after stories
  • 06 Product Feature: New drops, restocks, ring comparisons, "which ring matches your vibe"
  • 07 OT Explains: Caitlin's clinical knowledge made accessible, myth-busting, Q&A
  • 08 Regulation Tip: Quick actionable tips, sensory hacks, "try this when..." content
