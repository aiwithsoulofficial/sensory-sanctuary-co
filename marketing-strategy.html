02 / The Drop Model

Exclusive drops - how premium brands build demand

Instead of "everything always available" Shopify store energy, Sensory Sanctuary Co moves to a drop model. Limited collections. Announced dates. Waitlist signups. Sold-out energy. This does three things at once: builds the email list, creates urgency, and positions the brand as premium.

How it works

Week 1-2: Tease

Behind-the-scenes content. Rachel designing, choosing materials, testing the spin mechanism. Caitlin explaining the OT science behind the new piece. "Something's coming" energy. No product shots yet - just process and story.

Week 3: Reveal

Product reveal. Beautiful photography, close-up video, Rachel wearing it. Announce the drop date. Open the waitlist. "Join the waitlist to shop first" - this is the email capture moment.

Week 4: Warm the list

Email the waitlist with exclusive content - the story behind the ring name, the OT science, how to choose your size. Sell the digital products to the list while they wait. "While you wait for the drop, here's our Nervous System Regulation at Work guide - $0 for waitlist members."

Drop Day

Waitlist gets 24-hour early access. Then open to public. Countdown on socials. Rachel goes live wearing the ring. Limited stock messaging (if genuine). "3 hours in and the Dawn Ring is 60% gone."

Post-drop

Customer unboxing content. "First impressions" stories. "Sold out" badge on the website. Review collection emails. Start teasing the next drop. The cycle continues.

03 / The Funnel

List first. Digital second. Jewellery third.

The digital products are the secret weapon. They're low cost, high value, instant delivery, and they establish the OT credibility before anyone buys a ring. The funnel moves people from curious to educated to customer.

Discovery

TikTok / Instagram / Google search. Rachel's face, relatable hooks, OT education content. "The ring I spin under the table when my anxiety spikes."

Capture

"Join the next drop waitlist" or "Download our free Sensory Profile quiz." Email capture. They're on the list now.

Nurture with digital products

Welcome sequence sells the $0-15 digital products first. Nervous System Reset Ritual, Co-Regulation Guide, Workplace Regulation Guide. They experience the OT expertise before they buy jewellery.

The Drop

Waitlist gets early access to the ring drop. They already trust the brand, they've used the digital tools, they've been warmed through the sequence. Conversion is significantly higher.

Community & repeat

Post-purchase: review request, styling content, "wear it with" suggestions, referral program, next drop tease. Turn buyers into advocates.

Rachel's minimum commitment

She doesn't need to become a content creator. Here's the minimum that moves the needle:

- 2x per week: 30-second reel of her wearing the ring in a real setting (cafe, desk, driving). Can be filmed on her phone in natural light.

05 / Paid Strategy

Meta + TikTok Ads

Paid comes AFTER the organic foundation is set. No point running ads to a dead Instagram grid and a website with no positioning. Once the rebrand is live and there's 30+ pieces of quality content, paid amplifies what's already working.

Phase 1: List building ($15-25/day)

Objective: Waitlist signups

  • Platform: Meta (Facebook + Instagram) - broader targeting, lower CPL for list building
  • Ad format: Video - Rachel wearing the ring, 15-30 seconds, direct-to-camera or voiceover
  • Hook: "I'm a model. Nobody knows I have anxiety. This ring is how I get through it."
  • CTA: "Join the waitlist for our next drop" - leads to email capture landing page
  • Targeting: Women 22-45, interests: anxiety, ADHD, neurodivergent, occupational therapy, fidget toys, sensory processing, mental health awareness
  • Lookalike: Build from email list + website visitors once there's 100+ on the list
  • Expected CPL: $1.50-3.00 AUD for waitlist signup

Phase 2: Digital product sales ($20-30/day)

Objective: First purchase + pixel training

  • Platform: Meta + TikTok
  • Product: Free or $5 digital product (Nervous System Reset Ritual or Sensory Profile Quiz)
  • Why: Gets people through checkout, trains the pixel on buyers, establishes the OT credibility
  • Ad format: Caitlin explaining one tip from the guide, then "Download the full guide"
  • Targeting: Retarget waitlist + website visitors, cold targeting to anxiety/ADHD interests

Phase 3: Drop campaigns ($50-100/day, drop weeks only)

Objective: Ring sales during drops

  • Platform: Meta + TikTok + Google Shopping
  • Budget: Spike during drop week only - don't burn money between drops
  • Ad format: Product showcase video + Rachel wearing + urgency messaging
  • Retargeting: Waitlist who haven't purchased, website visitors, video viewers, digital product buyers
  • Cold: Best-performing organic content boosted as ads
  • Urgency: "Limited stock" / "Waitlist early access ends tonight" / "Sold out in the last drop"
  • Expected ROAS: 3-5x on retargeting, 2-3x on cold (once pixel is trained)

08 / Growth Levers

Beyond the first drop

NDIS eligibility

Kaiko Fidgets is NDIS-claimable and it transformed their business. If Sensory Sanctuary Co's products can be classified under NDIS Consumables or Assistive Technology, this opens a government-funded sales channel. With two OTs as founders, the clinical justification writes itself. Worth investigating with an NDIS provider consultant.

Wholesale / B2B

  • OT clinics and practices - sell as recommended tools for clients
  • Psychology and counselling practices
  • Corporate wellness programs
  • School and university wellbeing officers
